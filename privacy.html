Privacy policy

Effective 18 October 2026

This week is a weekly whiteboard for iPhone. It is built to keep your plans private.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking. It does not use the network.

Where your boards are stored

Your intentions, day notes, shopping list and settings are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app's own storage. They are included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) the same way as other app data, under your control.

Sharing

When you choose “Share this board” or share the shopping list, the app hands plain text to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next is your choice.

Deleting your data

Open the ••• menu and choose “Erase all data”, or delete the app. Both remove every saved week and the shopping list from the phone.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds accounts, sync or any data collection, this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated before that version is released.
